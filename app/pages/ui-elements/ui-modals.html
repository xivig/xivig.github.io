Enterprise Governance & SLA
1. Service Level Availability

Xivig guarantees a 99.99% monthly uptime availability for all edge routing fabrics, cluster orchestration nodes, and distributed microservices. Unscheduled downtime exceeding 0.01% entitles the customer to automatic billing credit adjustments.

2. Data Encryption & Zero-Trust Governance

All payloads transmitted across the fabric are protected with end-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption. Secret values stored in memory vaults are sealed using AES-256-GCM hardware security modules (HSM) with customer-managed keys.

3. Regional Compliance & GDPR

Data stored in regional deployments strictly adheres to European Union General Data Protection Regulations (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) standards.
