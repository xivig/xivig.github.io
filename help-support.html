Payment Solutions

Everything you need to know about managing your transactions and billing history.

Standard protocol involves a temporary authorization hold at the moment of checkout, but the final charge is typically finalized once the order is dispatched from our fulfillment center.

We support a full suite of secure payment options including Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, and PayPal. For eligible regions, digital wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay are also integrated.

Security is our priority. All transactions are processed through a PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant gateway using 256-bit SSL encryption. We never store your CVV or full card number on our servers.
